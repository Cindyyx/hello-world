About Me

I make products easier to use and processes faster to run. My habit is to put something real in front of people early, like a prototype, a simpler flow or a dashboard, and let what they do guide the next step.

That's how a four-step checkout became two, and three weeks of discovery became one. I've also led a CRM launch serving millions of customers and built AI workflows teams use every day, in French and English.

How I Work

Teammates describe me as organized, empathetic and analytical. Here is what that looks like in practice.

01

Show it before I spec it

I share a working prototype people can react to, then write the PRD from what they approved.

3→1weeks of discovery

02

Find the step where people quit

A 45% drop-off at step two told me where to cut. Checkout went from four steps to two.

+10%trial-to-paid conversion

03

Set the guardrails before the goal

When I repositioned a free trial, I watched paid retention, churn and app ratings, not just revenue.

+14%recurring revenue

04

It's not shipped until people use it

Before each release I trained the people who would use it, with bilingual guides and demos, and changed features based on what they told me.

40users trained per release
