Money rules

Split a bill by item

For one bill paid by one person, where people shared different dishes.

How to split by item

  1. In the expense, pick By item (beside Equal, Amount and Percent).
  2. Each item is a row: an optional name, the amount, and the faces of whoever had it. Tap a face to add or remove that person, or tap All.
  3. Tap + Item to add a row. When you switch to By item with no items yet, the first one is filled with the description and the amount before tax.
  4. A summary shows each person's part and who paid.

Scanning the receipt fills the item rows for you, so you only tap faces. If you scan while on another split, a hint offers to switch to By item.

Shared dishes, tax and tip

A dish shared by several people is split between them. Tax, service and the tip are then shared in proportion to what each person had.

How each part is worked outpart = bill total × (that person's items) ÷ (all items)

The parts are rounded once for the whole bill, so they always add up to the bill to the cent. The extra cent takes turns, like every split (see Splitting and cents).

Example: a $96.00 bill51.69 + 29.54 + 14.77 = 96.00

The Tax, Tip and Total lines

Right under the items, a box shows Tax, Tip and Total. You can edit each line by hand. When everything is shared out, it shows All split ✓; until then a line says how much of the bill isn't on an item yet.

Tax comes from the bill: what was charged, not what the app thinks it should be. See Tax and tip.

Adding a tip

Tap + Tip for a tip that wasn't on the itemized bill. The tip can be:

OptionWhat you type
Tip is in the bill (checkbox)The amount already includes the tip. Type how much it was.
An amountThe tip in money, added on top.
%A percent of the bill, before tax where tax is added at the till.
Card totalWhat your card was charged. The tip is that minus the bill.

The tip is optional. Unticking "Tip is in the bill" by mistake and ticking it again brings back the tip you typed.

Misread price check

When the items are a few cents off the subtotal, usually because one price was misread, a warning tells you:

WarningItems are $0.27 under the subtotal ($62.95). Check a price.

It says nothing while the list is still being typed (when the gap is over 10%).

How it's saved

A by-item expense is saved as an ordinary Amount split, with the item rows kept beside it so the form reopens in By item. If someone later changes it as an Amount split, it opens as that instead. Anything not on the receipt, like parking or a deposit, is its own expense.
