Invites and roles

Who can join a trip, and what each person is allowed to do in it.

Personal invite links

  • Each person in the trip has their own link.
  • Opening it joins the trip to your account. After that the link belongs to that account only, and nobody else can use it.
  • Someone already in the trip can't take another person's link.
  • An Admin can reset a link to free it, for example if the wrong person opened it.
A link is a secret until it's used. Anyone who opens a person's link before they do joins as that person. Send each link only to the person it's for.

Links that expire

An Admin can give a person's invite link an end date in Trip settings, under the link:

SettingWhat it means
Any timeThe default. The link never expires.
For 1 dayAfter the end date, nobody new can join with that link.
For 7 days
For 30 days

Whoever already joined with the link keeps using the trip on every device.

Roles

Roles are enforced by the database, not just the app.

RoleCan do
AdminEverything an Editor can, plus manage people, their access and their links.
EditorAdd and edit expenses.
ViewerSee the trip.

A payment is recorded by the person who got the money, or by an Admin. Viewers and Editors can ask for more access in Trip settings, and any Admin approves or declines it from a banner.

The trip Owner

  • The Owner is whoever created the trip, and always stays an Admin.
  • Only the Owner can delete or restore the trip. Deleted trips sit in Trash for 30 days.
  • To leave, the Owner hands the trip to another Admin first.
  • Other Admins can't demote or remove the Owner or reset their link. They can ask the Owner to hand the trip over to them.
