Settling

Settle up and payments

The fewest payments to make everyone even, and how paying someone back is recorded.

The fewest payments

Each person's balance is what the group owes them minus what they owe. Settle up turns those balances into the smallest number of transfers that makes everyone even.

Example4 people, 3 expenses → 2 payments, not 6

Mark paid

  1. Tap Mark paid on a payment once the money is sent.
  2. Change the amount for a partial payment or an overpayment.
  3. The card stays with Undo.

Every payment is listed under Payments recorded, with the expenses each is for. A wrong one is removed (kept on record) and entered again.

Paying back from the expense list

  • Tick one share to say that person paid the payer back.
  • Or use Select on expenses × people, or Mark all paid, with Undo.
  • One payment is one real transfer: marking many shares at once records one payment per person paying back each payer.

Where the ✓ marks come from

Ticks are worked out from payments. Money paid to a payer covers that person's shares of that payer's expenses first, oldest first. So paying for pre-trip bookings ticks those, while new expenses show as owed. A ✓ stays put when an older-dated expense is added later.

Editing or deleting an expense never loses a payment. When an edit changes the share of someone who already paid back, a note in the form says what happens to their money before you save. The Delete confirmation says the same.
