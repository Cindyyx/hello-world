Expenses

Scan receipts

Take or pick a photo and the expense fills itself in from what's printed on the bill.

What gets filled in

The amount, currency, date, shop, the shop's address and a category guess. The category guess lands on the trip's own category, even if it was renamed. For By item, the line items fill the item rows too.

Fields that came from the scan are highlighted so you can check them.

Two readers

ReaderWhen
Free AI reader (Google Gemini, free tier)Goes first when you're online and signed in. A scan waits for it up to 8 seconds.
Phone reader (text recognition on the device)Used when the AI can't answer: no signal, too slow, or its free daily allowance is used up. It downloads once, then works offline, and the photo stays on the phone.
Always free. The AI uses a Google project with no billing, so past its free daily limit it simply stops answering and the phone reader takes over. Nothing can be charged.

Printed amounts only

The scanner takes what's printed on the bill: tax, tip, total and items. It never works an amount out from a rate when the bill prints it. When a card slip's "Total" already includes the tax, the printed tax corrects the subtotal.

Card slip "Total 72.38" with tax 9.4362.95 + 9.43 = 72.38

Several photos of one receipt

A long receipt can be read in parts, top to bottom, in the order picked ("Reading part 1 of 2…"). Pick up to 4 photos at once from the library, or drop several on the tile. On Android, the Scan button offers Take photo or Choose from photos.

Languages

Besides English and French, the phone reader understands totals, subtotal, tax, tip, change lines, dishes and dates in Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Korean, Thai and Vietnamese, plus Russian, Turkish, Polish and Czech keywords. The AI reader reads any language and keeps item names as printed.

Dates like "21 de noviembre de 2026", 2026년 11월 21일, Japanese era years (令和8年), Taiwan Minguo years (115/11/21) and Thai Buddhist years (2569) are understood. Item counts like "合計 3点" are never taken as the total.

Extra reader languages download based on the trip currency and your phone's language, up to 4 at once so scans stay quick.
