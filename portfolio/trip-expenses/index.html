FAQ

Questions people ask

A personal project for friends and family. It runs on free hosting with no uptime guarantee, so please don't use it for business or sensitive financial records.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no card on file. It runs on free plans that pause rather than charge.

Do my friends need to install anything?

No. They open their invite link in a browser and sign in with Google. They can add it to their home screen if they want it to feel like an app.

What if we have no internet?

Keep adding expenses. They're stored on your phone and sync when you're back online. A small pill always shows whether your changes reached the cloud.

Where do exchange rates come from?

A free daily rates service. Each expense uses the rate for the day it was spent, and you can override it with the amount on your card statement.
